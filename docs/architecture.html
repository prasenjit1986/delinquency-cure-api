Gaps before production

  • Live transfer. Handoff today creates a case. Production adds Embedded Messaging and Omni-Channel so the chat moves to the officer live and the transcript links to the case. The Apex already sets MessagingSession.CaseId; that path is untested.
  • SMS activation. The segment exists; sending through a consented SMS channel with quiet hours is designed, not built.
  • Consent conflicts. Consent uses the "last updated" rule. With stale duplicate profiles that can pick an old "yes". Production should treat any recent "no" as no, or drop stale profiles at ingestion.
  • Speed. Median reply 6.1 s, p90 8.5 s, on a free Render instance with three calls in a row. Paid hosting and one combined summary-and-options call would cut most of it.
  • Pay now. Vera says a secure payment link will be sent; the payment link itself is not built.
  • Policy numbers. Windows, fees and limits are synthetic and must be confirmed against the credit union's real policy.
